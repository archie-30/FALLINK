## 繁體中文（zh-TW）

### 簡短說明（80 字內）
筆記本裡的手繪 3D 射擊 × 牌組構築，用墨水與卡牌打倒一頁又一頁的敵人。

### 完整說明（4000 字內）
整個世界，都畫在一本筆記本上。

INKRAGE 是一款手繪風 3D 射擊 × 牌組構築遊戲。鋼筆、鉛筆、橡皮擦、修正液……每一頁紙上都有等著你的塗鴉敵人與頭目。用搖桿移動與射擊，用墨水施放卡牌，在每一次的選擇中建立屬於你的牌組。

■ 手繪筆記本世界
所有角色、場景與特效都像用鋼筆和鉛筆畫在紙上，線條會微微抖動，彷彿仍在被畫著。

■ 射擊與卡牌合而為一
左搖桿移動、右搖桿瞄準與射擊，技能鍵就是你的手牌。散射、穿透筆、追蹤墨點、鉛筆牆、橡皮擦、紙盾、時停、分身……用有限的墨水決定下一張牌，打倒敵人還能補充墨水。

■ 筆記本模式
一共 3 幕，每一幕有多頁地圖與一場頭目戰。每一頁的門後都是不同的選擇：一般戰、精英戰、挑戰頁、寶箱、文具店、事件小遊戲、奇遇、修正液休息……先看清楚再選，走自己的路線。

■ 無盡模式
頁數無限、越來越難，每 5 頁遇到一位頭目，挑戰你的最高分。

■ 多種武器
鋼筆、自動鉛筆、毛筆、釘書機、螢光筆、圓規……升級解鎖，每把武器的手感都不同。

■ 組出你的牌組
獲得、升級、合成、甚至被撕掉卡牌。三張相同的牌可以合成強化版。

■ 訓練場與圖鑑
在訓練場自由嘗試卡牌，並隨時從圖鑑查看卡牌與敵人的資訊。

■ 還有更多
‧ 多款事件小遊戲：記憶音鈴、塗鴉靶場、描線練習、倒墨水、紙飛機、躲墨雨、猜杯子……
‧ 可自訂角色配色
‧ 支援繁體中文與 English
‧ 完全離線，不需要網路

■ 隱私
不需要登入，不蒐集個人資料，沒有廣告。

## English (en-US)

### Short description (80 chars max)
A hand-drawn 3D shooter × deckbuilder inside a notebook. Fight with ink and cards.

### Full description (4000 chars max)
The whole world is drawn in a notebook.

INKRAGE is a hand-drawn 3D shooter × deckbuilder. Fountain pens, pencils, erasers, correction fluid… every page holds doodle enemies and bosses waiting for you. Move and shoot with the twin sticks, cast cards with ink, and build your own deck with every choice you make.

■ A hand-drawn notebook world
Every character, set piece and effect looks pen-and-pencil drawn on paper, with lines that gently wobble as if still being sketched.

■ Shooting and cards in one
Left stick to move, right stick to aim and fire, and the skill buttons are your hand of cards. Scatter shot, piercing pen, homing ink dots, pencil walls, eraser, paper shield, time stop, clones… spend limited ink wisely, and defeat enemies to refill it.

■ Notebook mode
Three acts, each with several pages of maps and a boss fight. Every door leads somewhere different: normal fights, elites, challenge pages, treasure chests, a stationery shop, event mini-games, strange encounters, correction-fluid rests… scout first, then choose your route.

■ Endless mode
Unlimited pages, ever-rising difficulty and a boss every 5 pages. Chase your high score.

■ Many weapons
Fountain pen, mechanical pencil, brush, stapler, highlighter, compass… unlock them as you level up; each feels different.

■ Build your deck
Gain, upgrade, merge — or have cards torn up. Three identical cards merge into an upgraded one.

■ Training ground and codex
Try cards freely in the training ground and look up any card or enemy in the codex.

■ And more
‧ Event mini-games: memory bells, doodle shooting range, line tracing, ink pouring, paper planes, ink-rain dodging, cup guessing…
‧ Customisable character colours
‧ Traditional Chinese and English
‧ Fully offline, no internet needed

■ Privacy
No sign-in, no personal data collected, no ads.

- 暴力：有卡通風格的墨水射擊與幻想暴力，**沒有血腥寫實畫面**（受傷時是紅色墨水暈邊）。
